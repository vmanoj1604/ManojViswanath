Each model is accompanied by immersive audio explanations, offering a rich, interactive learning experience that makes understanding the cosmos both engaging and fun.

3. Markerless AR Game

Experience a markerless AR adventure where ghosts roam freely in your real world. Collect points as you encounter them, with each capture boosting your score in this immersive and interactive experience.

The Human Anatomy Lab is a cutting-edge Virtual Reality (VR) experience designed to provide an immersive tour of a high-tech anatomy laboratory. Users can explore a realistic 3D environment and learn about the human body in an engaging and educational way.

5. ForkLift VR Simulation

Experience forklift operation in a fully immersive virtual environment. This VR simulation is designed to train users in safe and efficient forklift handling, featuring lifelike physics and interactive controls.

The Engine Assembly VR Setup is an immersive and interactive Virtual Reality (VR) training experience designed to teach users the step-by-step process of assembling an engine in a realistic virtual environment. This simulation enhances learning by providing hands-on experience without the need for physical components, making it ideal for engineering students, mechanics, and automotive professionals.
